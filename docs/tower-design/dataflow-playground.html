How to read it

Orientation = arity. An up-triangle takes one signal in at its base and emits two up its sides. A down-triangle takes two in and merges to one. Because the lattice forces ▲▽ to alternate, every shape is automatically a rhythm of split → merge → split.
● sink (output)   ● source (core feed)   ○ candidate edge — click to toggle   → internal flow
Routing conserves every stat. The base shot (power = core − crystal draw, plus base rate/speed/range) is divided among the sources by their weights, then routed: a ▲ divides each stat among its outputs, a ▽ sums each stat. Each crystal multiplies/modifies on top, and the weapon = the sum of all sinks. So a pure-wire shape fires the base shot unchanged; operators are the only amplifiers. Damage = power × rate.
