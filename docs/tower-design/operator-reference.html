Crystal Operator Reference

Every crystal has two facets — a ▲ split (the lighter, spread version) and a ▽ merge (the heavier "focus" version, acting on the summed stream). Orientation is set by where the crystal sits on the lattice, so the shape decides which facet you get. Edit a number and the formula + example update live; values are saved and shared with the playground.

Sample shot
How facets fit the model: the base shot is divided among the sources by weight, then routed — routing conserves every stat (▲ divides each stat among outputs, ▽ sums them). A crystal then applies its facet ON TOP: a ▲ uses its split effect, a ▽ uses its merge effect. Because a ▽ acts on the already-summed (concentrated) stream and usually carries the stronger numbers, where power converges is the core puzzle. Damage = power × rate. Quartz is identity in both forms — a free wire.
