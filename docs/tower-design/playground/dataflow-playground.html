Crystal Dataflow — Triangle Lattice Explorer

Left-click a cell to place a crystal (▲ splits 1→2, ▽ merges 2→1). Flow runs upward. Right-click a boundary crystal to cycle its role: green = a weighted source (core seeds this crystal), orange = a sink (drains to the weapon). A crystal fed by another crystal can't be a source; one feeding another can't be a sink.

How to read it

Orientation = arity. An up-triangle takes one signal in at its base and emits two up its sides. A down-triangle takes two in and merges to one. Because the lattice forces ▲▽ to alternate, every shape is automatically a rhythm of split → merge → split.
▣ source crystal (core seeds it)   ▣ sink crystal (drains to weapon)   ○ candidate — right-click to set role   → internal flow
Sources & sinks live on boundary crystals, not edges. A source seeds the whole crystal (its input arity is ignored); a sink collects the crystal's output. Terminals only sit where the crystal has a free side — leaf-input / leaf-output.
Energy is conserved by local tolls. The full core energy is divided among the sources by their weights, then routed. Each crystal draws its own cost from the flowing energy as the stream passes it; a ▲ divides the remainder among its outputs, a ▽ sums its inputs. The energy arriving at a crystal (floored at 0) is the multiplier for the combo-op it forms with its upstream neighbor — so front crystals fire on more energy than deep ones. Energy below 0 is debt (the op does nothing until a ▽ merge sums it back positive). Adjacent crystals form combo-ops (named in the combo matrix); the weapon energy = the sum of all sinks.

Ops produced

Each adjacent crystal pair produces a combo-op (matrix), scaled by the energy reaching the downstream crystal. Names are stubs — behavior is authored under effect-vocab/ops/.

Shot payload (op-flow)

Ops ride the flow as a bag of (op → quantity): ▲ splits divide each quantity, ▽ merges sum them. An interactive op consumes the primitive it reacts to (1-to-1) and emits itself. Each sink's bag is what that shot delivers.
