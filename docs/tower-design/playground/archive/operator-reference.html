Crystal Combo Reference

Two crystals adjacent in a tower's dataflow produce a combo-op, scaled by the energy reaching the downstream crystal. This is a read-only mirror of combo-matrix.md (the source of truth). Op names are stubs — each op's behavior is authored one-per-file under effect-vocab/ops/ and implemented later in the Godot / C# port. Used as the lookup by the dataflow playground.

Combo matrix

Diagonal (shaded) = a crystal's single-crystal native op, produced when two of that crystal sit adjacent. Off-diagonal = a two-crystal op. The grid is symmetric — order does not matter. Click any op to open its stub doc.
